# NavBar

The site header: the Techrity lavender lock-up, then nav links (About · Get Involved ▾ · Opportunities · Events · Blog), then **Shop** (outline + cart) and **Donate** (primary).

- **Co-brand:** on Tech for Good pages, pass `cobrandSrc` for the T4G mark. It sits after a hairline divider, matching the family rule *Techrity first*.
- **Active link:** brand purple (`aria-current="page"`). Hover is also purple.
- **You supply:** `logoSrc` (the Lockup Lavender asset), `links`, `dropdowns`, `active`, and `ctaLabel` (default "Donate").
