# StoryCard

A participant story for the **People** and **Progress** narratives: programme label, photo, the quote in the participant's words, an **Impact:** line stating the change, and **View Full Story**.

- **Content:** quote in first person. The Impact line states the outcome, not the activity ("Transitioned from retail to full-stack development"). Get consent before using names and photos, especially of Young, Bold & Digital participants (13–18).
- **You supply:** `programme`, `imageSrc` / `imageAlt`, `quote`, `impact`, `author`, `href`. Without an image, a flat `lavender-tint` placeholder holds the space. Always supply a real, consented photo before publishing.
