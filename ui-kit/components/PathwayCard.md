# PathwayCard

A programme entry card for the ecosystem pathways: a number, the programme name, one line on what it gives, and an up-right arrow. It mirrors *One ecosystem, multiple entry points*.

- **Tones:** `gray` (`ui-gray`, default) and `purple` (`brand-purple`). Gray turns purple on hover or focus. White text is 7.8:1 on gray and 8.9:1 on purple; numerals are `ui-cyan`.
- **Content:** use the stage order (01 Young, Bold & Digital, 02 Kickstart, 03 TMP …) and a single benefit line, e.g. "Access a laptop, data & foundational support".
- **You supply:** `index`, `title`, `description`, `tone`, `href`.
